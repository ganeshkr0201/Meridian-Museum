TITLE: Curator Note — Mechanical Engineering Models: Gear Trains and Water Power
AUTHOR: Museum Curatorial Team
COLLECTION: Meridian Museum of World Cultures — Fictional Demonstration Dataset

The water-powered mill model on display demonstrates a horizontal paddle-wheel driving a vertical shaft through a pair of bevel gears. The gear ratio of 1:4 steps up rotational speed to grind grain efficiently. The fictional Meridian engineers who designed this system described it in a manuscript titled "On the Turning of Mills" (fictional reference MAN-07-A). The printing press model adjacent to it uses a screw mechanism to generate approximately 300 kilograms of downward force from a modest hand-lever effort, illustrating mechanical advantage principles accessible to secondary and tertiary students.
