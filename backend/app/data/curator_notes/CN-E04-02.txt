TITLE: Curator Note — Traditional Textiles: Social Significance
AUTHOR: Museum Curatorial Team
COLLECTION: Meridian Museum of World Cultures — Fictional Demonstration Dataset

In the fictional Halcyon Region, textile patterns communicated social status, clan affiliation, and ceremonial role. Marriage garments used a distinctive double-diamond chevron border that is not found on everyday wear. Funerary wrappings used a plain white ground cloth with a single red selvedge line. The colour indigo was reserved for elder craftspeople and community leaders, while natural-dyed ochre patterns were accessible to all social strata. These conventions are documented in fictional ethnographic records held by the Halcyon Archive, cited in catalogue entry CAT-E04.
